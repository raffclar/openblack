# Bugs in vanilla Black & White

openblack replicates vanilla Black & White exactly, bugs included, unless the user decides otherwise. This file lists
every bug or quirk found in vanilla (version 1.20, the BW1W120 executable), with its evidence, what it does in play, and
what openblack does about it.

- **Kept** means openblack does what vanilla does. Nothing goes in `deviations.md`.
- **Fixed** means openblack behaves differently on purpose. That is a deviation: the row here links its row in
  `deviations.md`, where the user's sign-off is recorded.
- **Guarded** means vanilla would crash or read invalid memory. openblack logs vanilla's message (if it has one) and
  carries on without the bad read. Nothing visible changes in play, so it is not a deviation.
- **Open** means it is recorded but nothing is decided or built yet.

Rows are only appended or edited in place, never renumbered or deleted. The `openblack-vanilla-bugs` skill describes
how to find, record and decide them.
